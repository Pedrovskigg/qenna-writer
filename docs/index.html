What is Qenna Writer?

A program for writing long fiction — novels and screenplays — from the first draft through the revision. Chapters, scenes, characters, worldbuilding and timeline in the same place, instead of scattered across four different programs.

A thriller with details that can't be forgotten? A twelve-book fantasy saga? A space opera with a tightly wound political plot?

Whichever one is yours. Qenna handles it — and handles it well.

Solarized Highlands

I  The editorQenna Writer

A chapter three books ago is one click away

The Reference panel opens beside the page: any chapter, scene or sheet held open next to what you're writing, without switching windows and without losing the thread. Chapters split into scenes, and the scenes show up in the sidebar as sub-items.

  • Several manuscripts in one project — draft a whole trilogy without losing track of book one.
  • Drawers for characters, places, objects and lore, with a search that sweeps the entire drawer.
  • @ mentions linking characters and terms across documents.
  • Screenplay mode with the real elements — Scene, Action, Character, Dialogue, Parenthetical, Transition.
  • Repetition detector flagging repeated words, adverb pile-ups and paragraphs that ran too long.
II  The whole manuscript at onceQenna Writer

Every chapter becomes a card, with the numbers inside it

Outline Mode opens the manuscript as bands of cards: how each chapter starts, its word count, how many lines of dialogue, the split between dialogue and narration, and who's on stage. None of that is a field you fill in — it's read from what you already wrote.

Up top: 5 chapters · 15 scenes · 2,873 words — added up on their own.

III  Who's on stageQenna Writer

Know who showed up, where, and who said what

The Characters drawer holds a sheet, a photo and a role for each one. Qenna cross-checks that against the text and counts on its own: how many scenes a character appears in, how many lines they have, who they share the page with most.

  • Presence detection per chapter and per scene.
  • Relationship graph built from who actually shows up together.
  • Searchable dialogue log — every line, sorted by who said it.
  • Reminders to flag what's left loose and come back before it goes cold.
IV  Before the first sentenceQenna Writer

A place for the parts that aren't prose yet

The Planning Board is a blank canvas: post-its, sheets, images and linked documents connected however the plot actually connects — not a mind-map with fixed nodes. Any card becomes a timeline event later.

V  Make it yoursQenna Writer

286 themes, or one built from scratch

The page colour and the panel colour are independent — you can have a dark interface wrapped around a white page, or the other way around. You can set a day theme and a night theme and let Qenna switch on a schedule.

  • Search and favourites in the gallery — the heart marks the ones you keep coming back to.
  • Export and import a theme: a theme becomes a file and travels to another machine, or to another person.
  • Automatic switching by time of day between a day theme and a night theme.
  • Theme editor to duplicate a finished one and tinker, or build from nothing.
  • Ambient sound built in, no browser tab full of rain.
  • Movable bars — the toolbar goes wherever you'd rather have it.
StatisticsQenna Writer

The project's numbers, counted on their own

The Statistics panel asks you to fill in nothing. It reads the manuscript and hands back each character's participation, the size of every chapter and a summary of the whole project — all of it from the text that's already there. It answers the question no ordinary editor answers: where is this book out of balance?

  • Presence per character, as a share of scenes — you can see at a glance who vanished from act two.
  • Words per chapter as bars, with the biggest and smallest named.
  • Chemistry: who shares the page with whom, counted in scenes, chapters and lines.
  • Lines and words spoken per character, kept apart from narration.
  • Not one of those fields is filled in by hand.

License

The Qenna Writer License, based on GPLv3. Anyone can read, change and redistribute this code for free — nobody can turn it into a closed, or a paid, product.

One person builds and maintains this in spare time. Bugs get filed on GitHub Issues and are usually fixed in days, not quarters. There's no support queue to wait in.

Contact

To report bugs, suggest features, or just scream about your plot at 3:00 AM: qennawriter@gmail.com. It can take me a few hours or a few days to answer, but I will.
