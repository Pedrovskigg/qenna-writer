## What's new

**○ ANIMATED THEMES!**
That's right! Qenna now has a new category of Themes. Animated Themes.
That's 55 Themes with vivid illustrations that move, with effects, rain, snow, lightning storms and much more!
Seriously. There are lots of cool options, not just in the Themes themselves but in the ways to customize them!

**•** Customize for each theme: time of day, rain and layers on top (stars, fog, leaves, petals, fireflies, snow). Saved per theme.
**•** Real time: the light follows your computer's clock. In several themes the hours pass inside the artwork: day turns into dusk and then night, little by little.
**•** The thumbnails and the preview in the Themes panel show the weather, plus the options to customize it.
**•** In Settings: on/off, intensity, animation quality and frames per second, for weaker computers.

The animation pauses on its own when Qenna is in the background or minimized.

And turning to the Board now:

**○ Pen and Sketch on the Board!**
Now, if you have a pen display or a pen tablet, or your computer supports a pen in any way, you can use it right on the Board.
There's a pen, a marker, a highlighter and an eraser, and it picks up the pen's pressure.
No pen? It works with the mouse too.
And a couple of tricks: hold still at the end of a stroke and it becomes a perfect shape, and with Shift you get a straight line.

And for the artists out there:
**• Sketch**
Inside the Board, you can use the Sketch tool. A blank page with layers and brushes to draw with.
You can export the artwork, and the app imports a few external brush formats.
And the drawing can become your character's photo.

Also arriving:
**○ Name autocorrect**
A practical example makes this one easier to explain.
If your character's name is Mary and you type Marg, the app will automatically correct it to Mary.
It works for characters, settings, objects and nicknames. The first Backspace right after undoes it and teaches the word to the dictionary. Turn it on or off in Settings › Spell checker.

*And now, back to the workbench.*

## Fixes and tweaks

**• Project cover**
"Quick cover" and "Load cover" split along a diagonal, in the New project and Edit project sheets. The synopsis box in New project got much bigger (about 12 lines).

**• Builder rename**
Renaming in the Builder and in Territories now respects the whole word. Before, renaming a name that appeared inside another one changed both: renaming Ana also changed Mariana. Fixed.

**• Smooth cursor**
The letter fade effect of the smooth cursor no longer disappears when you go back to edit the middle of a sentence.

**• Timeline**
The date labels in the Braid no longer run over each other.
