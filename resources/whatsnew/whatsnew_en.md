**Qenna Writer - Update 2.5**

![Qenna's Goofy kicking the door down: "IT'S QENNA UPDATE TIME!"](qrc:/whatsnew/update-time.png)

Version 2.5 of Qenna Writer is here to smooth out some rough edges from past versions and, as always, bring new stuff too!
We're paying extra attention to the Mac versions! And more bugs were fixed over there.
But without further ado, let's go:

## What's new

I'll start this section with a new feature that I'll admit right away: nobody asked for it.
It's a feature I decided to put in Qenna because I missed writing this way and having this freedom.

*So... I give you:*

**• Full Manuscript**

With this on, you can read, edit, write and even work from the very beginning, if you want, seeing the whole book in the editor. No need to keep switching between chapters through the drawers or shortcuts. It's all on screen. Just scroll.
Here's some extra info about this toy:
1. Writing, pasting, searching, undo, redo, etc. Everything works across the whole book and each thing stays in its own chapter. This mode can be toggled, and your edits are still saved in the individual chapters they belong to.
2. Chapter openings are protected. In other words, chapters never merge into each other, no matter what.
3. With this mode on, the Drawer becomes a table of contents. Clicking a chapter scrolls the editor to it. Shift+PgUp/PgDown jump between them.

The optimization is good. One of my biggest projects, a raw novel with 192 thousand words, opens in this mode in about 1 second. It's smooth. Of course, that may vary depending on your machine and all that, but it'll flow well. The editor itself took no performance hit and is still as fluid and nice to write in as it's always been and always will be.

*And also arriving now...*

**• Customization: Qenna's Bars**

I got tired of the toolbar (the top bar). Simple as that. It started looking way too cluttered to me, with too many buttons and way too long. And after trying to redo it over and over, I ended up with several different options that served different needs and looks.
And of course, I thought: "Why not?"

Qenna now has **7 different styles** of top bars. Bars that look like Word, for example. Traditional, minimalist, and the rotating one (which became the new default bar).
Don't worry, if you don't like the new one, the classic bar can still be brought back in the settings.

The same goes for the explorer bar (the left bar): it now has **4 new styles** too, switchable in the appearance section of the settings. I didn't touch the explorer bar as much because, unlike the toolbar, it's good. It always worked well and never caused trouble.

And also, this is new:

**World bar (right bar)**
To take some weight off the toolbar's back, I went with a new alternative: a right bar. It's small, discreet and... well, it's on the right (wow, who would've thought?).
Reference Menu, Pensarium, Reminders, World Builder, AI Assistant and Statistics were moved to it.

And of course, along with all of Qenna's bars, you can now change how long they are on screen too.

On top of that, there's more:
1. Carousel
The bar icons scroll when there isn't room for all of them to fit.
2. Embossed bars
Turn it on in the appearance settings: the bars become translucent, with the buttons embossed and very discreet. It keeps the UI cleaner when your focus is only on the text. I recommend it, it's really comfortable.

And now, still on the UI, we have:
*The only one that hadn't been touched by the latest waves of visual reworks... until now.*

**• NEW IMMERSIVE SOUND PANEL**

Yes, it's here!
The new Immersive Sound panel is available in this version. A redone UI, pretty, illustrated and easy to navigate.
And on top of that, it brought an amazing new tool:

**Mix Mode**
Now, in Immersive Sound, you can put two sounds together and mix them quick and easy to build even cozier atmospheres!
Rain with a horror piano in the background?
A pretty guitar by Ebunny over the crackling of a campfire?
City traffic near the beach?

All of it is easy to do and takes a few clicks. Immersive Sound is now on the next level FOR REAL! And believe me, it's amazing.

*And still on the UI*

**• New Counter Panel**
Our daily progress panel and the counter got a makeover!
The new panel is prettier and easier to navigate. It's now split into tabs, to see your goals, start sprints and follow your month calendar.

To the veterans, don't worry: the calendar stars are still untouched.

**• Export Panel redone**
Qenna's export panel was redone too. Now with a preview, easier to navigate and to choose what gets exported.

And now, to the editor tweaks:

**• Document Tabs**
Even faster and more direct navigation!
Your open documents in the editor can now be navigated between. You can go back to documents that were open, quick and easy.
Alt + left/right arrow: go back or forward through the open documents.
Or you can move the mouse to the bottom right corner and browse the tabs of the open documents. Oh! It also works with your mouse's side buttons, if yours has them.

**• Selection menu tweak**
The editor's selection menu (the one that shows up when you select some text) was huge.
I added a new feature that makes it smaller, with the buttons moving inside it like a carousel.

**• Page and Margins**
The page and margin settings were brought to the top bar.

**• Chapter marker**
The chapter title in the top strip got new designs, switchable with a right-click. They also show up separating the chapters in full book mode.

And now, moving on to the map...

**• NEW: Create a map from an image**
A suggestion I got, and it made sense. If you already have a map of your story and want to use the map tools, you can! You can now load a map as an image. Some tools don't work in this mode, but all the main ones do.

**• New sprites on the map**
New sprites added: castles, statues, buildings and brushes.

**• New: even smaller world scale**
There's now a new option to make the world scale EVEN SMALLER than the previous minimum.
That makes the world huge, even on medium maps. On a supercontinent? You can rebuild all of Brazil and still have dozens of empty kilometers left over.

And now, for the weekly housekeeping:

## Fixes and smaller tweaks

1. Fixed a bug in the Theme creator where the UI text color wasn't sticking.
2. Each document now saves exactly where your text cursor stopped.
3. Fixed a COLOSSAL design flaw where the left bar simply didn't touch the corner of the screen, and it was also slimmed down for a cleaner interface (button size untouched).
4. New focused editor icon.
5. Still in focused editor, fixed a bug where the left bar could disappear even with your mouse on it.
6. Fixed how documents load, so now all of them open faster, even the huge ones.
7. Map text could get pixelated when the map was exported. Fixed.
8. Blurry theme photos on Mac (Retina conflict) or on Windows (conflict with interfaces above 125% size). Fixed.
9. When using images in chapters, the title strip could keep the color of the previous chapter. Fixed.
10. Interface trails on Mac when opening, closing or moving things around the screen. Fixed.
11. COLOSSAL BUG: if the text was very long, you could "overflow" the scroll limit. In short, even with the screen scrolled all the way down, there could still be text below. Fixed.
12. Fixed a bug in page mode where Ctrl+Z could remove the margin.
13. While browsing the settings, the mouse wheel could change settings by accident. Fixed.
